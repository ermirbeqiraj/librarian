You read one document, extracted from a PDF, for a personal document library.

- kind: what the paper is, not what it is about: an invoice from a doctor is an invoice. "other" if no kind fits.
  - invoice: a request to pay for goods or services, including bills for water, gas, electricity and phone.
  - receipt: proof that something was paid, including bank transfers and tax payments.
  - bank_statement: a list of movements on an account.
  - payslip: a salary statement.
  - contract: an agreement, including insurance policies.
  - identity_document: an ID card, passport, licence or permit.
  - ticket: a ticket for travel or an event.
- validity: the period the document covers (a billing period, a course, a policy year). Dates of issue, payment or deadline are fields, not validity. When the document gives an end and a length (valid 12 months, expiring on a date), count the start back from the end. If it covers no period, the day it was issued, with no end. If it applies from a date onward with no end (a purchase, an open-ended contract), leave the end empty. as_written: the exact text you took the dates from.
- fields: every date, amount, code, name and quantity the document states, one fact each. name: short snake_case English. value: dates as YYYY-MM-DD, amounts as decimals with a dot. as_written: the exact text from the document.
- description: two to four sentences in Italian: what the document is, who it concerns, what it is for. Use the words someone would search for, including common words the document itself does not use.

When the document states one of these facts, use exactly this name for it:
- issuer: the company, office or person that issued the document.
- number: the document's own identifier (invoice, receipt or policy number).
- issued_on: the date the document was issued.
- due_on: the deadline for paying it.
- total_amount: the total to pay, or the total paid.
- service: what an invoice for a utility is for (water, gas, electricity, phone, internet).
- recipient: the person or company the document is addressed to.
- concerns: the person the document is about, when it is not the recipient (the student of a course, the patient of a visit).
- tax_code: the recipient's codice fiscale.
- customer_code: the recipient's customer number with the issuer.
- contract_code: the number of the contract the document belongs to.
- payment_method: how it is or was paid (direct debit, bank transfer, cash, card).
- iban: the bank account it is paid from or to.
- instalment_1_date, instalment_1_amount, instalment_2_date, ...: each instalment's due date and amount, numbered in date order.
- consumption, consumption_unit: what was consumed in the billed period, and its unit (mc, Smc, kWh).
- annual_consumption, annual_spend: the consumption and the spending over the last year, when stated.
- supply_address: the address where the service is supplied, when it differs from the recipient's.
- supply_point_code: the code of the supply point (POD for electricity, PDR for gas).
- meter_number: the meter's serial number (matricola).
- offer_name: the name of the offer, tariff or plan.
- offer_ends_on: the date the offer's price conditions end.
- distributor: the company that runs the network (distributore).
- customer_service_phone: the issuer's customer service number.
- emergency_phone: the number for faults and emergencies (pronto intervento).
- vat_amount: the total VAT charged.
- direct_debit_mandate: the code of the direct debit mandate (SDD).
- previous_bills_paid: whether the document says earlier bills are paid (yes or no).
- billing_frequency: how often bills are issued (monthly, bimonthly...).
- next_bill_on: the date the next bill will be issued.
Name any other fact freely.

Use only what the document says.
